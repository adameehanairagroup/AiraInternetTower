# Legal notes (India) - not legal advice, verify with DoT/WPC
- Mobile bands are auctioned to operators. Unlicensed transmission is illegal.
- Lab testing: use a shielded box or RF cables with attenuators, minimum TX power, test PLMN 001/01.
- Experimental/research: apply for a WPC experimental licence.
- Private/captive 5G for enterprises: DoT process exists (direct spectrum assignment) - check current rules.
- Licence-free: 2.4 GHz and parts of 5 GHz Wi-Fi (power limits apply) -> use for mesh and point-to-point.
- Public Wi-Fi: PM-WANI framework (PDO/PDOA/App provider/Central registry). KYC + logging rules apply.
- Kerala: check K-FON local status (BPL free connections) with your panchayat.
- Do not share your personal ISP connection publicly against its terms.
